# Bayani Retail Corp. — Attendance Policy (excerpt)

- Work starts at 8:00 AM. Arriving after 8:10 AM counts as late.
- Habitual tardiness: 6 or more late arrivals within 60 days.
- First finding of habitual tardiness: written warning, after a Notice to Explain and a chance to answer.
- An employee caring for a sick family member may ask HR for flexible hours before a penalty is decided.
