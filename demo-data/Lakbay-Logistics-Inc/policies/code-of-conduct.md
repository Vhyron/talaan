# Lakbay Logistics Inc. — Code of Conduct (excerpt)

## Section 4. Company property
4.1 Removing company stock or property from any site without a signed gate pass is a serious offense.
4.2 First offense: suspension of up to 30 days or dismissal, depending on value and intent, after due process.

## Section 7. Due process
7.1 Every charge starts with a Notice to Explain (NTE) giving the employee at least five (5) calendar days to answer in writing.
7.2 The employee may attend an administrative hearing with a representative of their choice.
7.3 A written Notice of Decision follows the hearing. It must state the facts and the evidence relied on.
7.4 Approved leave records and medical certificates are evidence and must be considered.

## Section 9. Records
9.1 Investigation files are confidential. Witnesses may ask that their identity be withheld from the employee.
9.2 Copies of case documents are released to the employee's representative only on written request.
